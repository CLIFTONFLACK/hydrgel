Cookies and analytics

Last updated: 4 October 2026.

Who runs this site

This site is run by HYDRGEL PTE. LTD., 21 Science Park Rd, #01-8, Singapore 117628. Questions about this notice or your data: info@hydrgel.com.

What we measure, and only if you say yes

If you press Accept in the cookie banner, we use Google Analytics 4 to count visits to hydrgel.com and www.hydrgel.com: which pages are viewed, roughly where from (country level), device and browser type, and how you arrived (the site that referred you, reduced to its address). We use it to see which pages are useful. Nothing is sent to Google before you accept. Pages measured: the pages of the website. This notice page is never measured.

What we switch off

Advertising features, ad personalisation and Google signals are off. The address of each page is sent without its query string (except campaign tags that start with utm_) and without any fragment. Referrers from this same site are not sent.

Cookies

Accepting sets these on this site only (each host, hydrgel.com or www.hydrgel.com, keeps its own): _ga and _ga_<container id> (tell visits apart; Google Analytics keeps them up to 2 years). Your choice itself is stored in your browser’s local storage under gb_consent (“granted” or “denied”), not in a cookie, and never leaves your device. Declining sets no analytics cookies.

Changing your mind

Use the “Cookie settings” button on any page of the website at any time. Choosing Decline switches the tag off and deletes the analytics cookies immediately.

Google

Google acts as our processor for analytics; data may be processed outside the UK/EEA under Google’s safeguards. Data retention in Google Analytics is set to 14 months. See Google’s own policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Other data

The contact and enquiry forms collect what you type into them (such as your name, email address, organisation and message). Depending on how the form is set up, it either opens your email program with the message prepared for you to send, or sends it to us through a form service. Separately from analytics, the site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, and the Humanitarian page embeds a YouTube video, so your browser contacts Google for those whatever you choose in the cookie banner, and YouTube may set its own cookies when the video loads. Contact us at info@hydrgel.com about any other data.
